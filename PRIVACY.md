# Beam privacy policy

_Last updated: October 7, 2026_

Beam is a desktop app for sending files directly between computers on the same local network.
This policy explains what Beam does with information. In short: **Beam does not collect, sell or
share any personal data, and has no servers.**

## What Beam does not do

- Beam does not create accounts and does not ask for your name, e-mail address or any other
  personal details.
- Beam does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking or telemetry of any kind.
- Beam does not upload your files, file names or any other data to the internet or to the
  developer. There are no Beam servers.

## Information that stays on your computer

To work, Beam stores the following **only on your own computer**, in its app data folder:

- The name you give your computer in Beam, your settings (for example the folder received files
  are saved to) and the list of computers you chose to trust.
- A device identifier and an encryption key that let other computers recognise yours. The key is
  protected with Windows' built-in data protection.
- A history of recent transfers (direction, computer name, file or folder name, size, time and
  result). You can clear it at any time in Beam > History > Clear history.
- Log files with technical details for troubleshooting, kept for 7 days.

Beam never keeps copies of the files you send or receive.

## Information shared with nearby computers

When Beam is open, it announces your computer's Beam name, a device identifier and network address
on your **local network** so that other computers running Beam can find it. You can turn this off
in Settings > "Let nearby computers find this one".

When you send files, the names, sizes and contents of the files you selected go **directly** to the
computer you chose, over an encrypted connection. When someone sends you files, nothing is saved
unless you accept.

## Your choices

- Hide your computer from others (Settings).
- Clear transfer history (History).
- Remove all Beam data by uninstalling Beam and deleting its app data folder.

## Children

Beam does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
